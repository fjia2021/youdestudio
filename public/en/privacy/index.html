This website is served via Cloudflare (Cloudflare Workers), and the domain youdestudio.com is managed through Cloudflare (DNS). When you visit the pages, the provider processes technically necessary connection data, in particular your IP address, date and time, requested page and browser information (server log files). Data may be transferred to the USA; according to Cloudflare this is based on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses. More information: Cloudflare privacy policy.

4. Email forwarding via Cloudflare

Emails to the address published on this website are handled by Cloudflare Email Routing, which forwards incoming messages to a private mailbox. Message content and metadata are processed technically for this purpose. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

5. Contact by email or contact form

If you email me or use the contact form in the legal notice, I process your details (name, email address, message content) solely to handle your enquiry. Messages sent through the form are transmitted by a Cloudflare Worker and delivered to my private mailbox via Cloudflare Email; they are not stored on the website itself. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (pre-contractual measures) or Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. The data is deleted once it is no longer needed for that purpose and no statutory retention duties apply.

6. External links

This website links to external sites (for example Google Scholar). Once you follow a link, the privacy policy of the respective provider applies.

7. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and objection (Art. 21). You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection authority, for example the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information Baden-Württemberg, Lautenschlagerstraße 20, 70173 Stuttgart, Germany.
