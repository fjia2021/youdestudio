Selected simulation work

Projects from my research at University Medical Center Freiburg, each carried from concept through mathematical formulation, implementation and experimental validation to publication.

Non-linear gradient coil for breast diffusion MRI

Insert coil with a local gradient above 1 T/m, more than ten times that of a clinical whole-body scanner. Measured field maps matched the simulation.

J Magn Reson 2021

Shim coil array matched to brain anatomy

Array designed from in-vivo field maps of 18 subjects, with SVD-based element reduction to cut the channel count.

Magn Reson Med 2020

PNS safety for interventionalists

First assessment of peripheral nerve stimulation exposure of staff working inside the scanner bore during image-guided procedures.

MAGMA 2025

Also: shielded matrix gradient coil elements for non-linear spatial encoding, gradient coils for a low-cost teaching scanner, and topology optimisation of small-scale gradient coils. Full publication list on Google Scholar.
